# al marifah

Build a modern, high-performance web platform in English named "Alma'rifa" (meaning "The Knowledge"), designed for publishing articles, essays, poetry, and diverse literary genres. This website is the official English version of "Ainul-Haqq" (ainul-haqq.com).

### 1. Design & Aesthetic Principles

- Orientation: Left-to-Right (LTR), English-first layout.

- Styling: Elegant, clean, typography-focused, editorial look suitable for long-form reading, literature, and scholarly articles.

- Color Palette: Deep navy/emerald green accents combined with warm off-white, light gray backgrounds, and crisp dark typography. Support light and dark themes.

- Typography: Premium serif fonts for body text (e.g., Playfair Display or Merriweather) and clean sans-serif for navigation and metadata (e.g., Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans).

### 2. Header & Navigation

- Top Bar: Language toggle (linking back to Ainul-Haqq Arabic site), dark mode switcher, and search bar with dynamic auto-complete.

- Brand Section: Logo displaying "Alma'rifa" in an elegant typography style with a subtle tagline ("A Hub for Thought, Literature, and Knowledge").

- Primary Navigation Menu:

  * Home

  * Articles & Essays

  * Poetry

  * Literary Genres (Short Stories, Reviews, Perspectives)

  * About Us

  * Contact / Submissions

### 3. Homepage Layout

- Hero Feature Section: A multi-column showcase for top-trending or editorial picks featuring prominent images, category tags, author names, read-time estimates, and published dates.

- Category Grid / Sections:

  * Featured Essays & Long-form Analysis

  * Poetry Corner (special dynamic layout with centered/formatted stanzas and atmospheric ambient design)

  * Recent Articles (card-based layout with hover effects)

  * Editor's Choice & Opinion Pieces

- Interactive Media/Sidebar:

  * Most Read / Popular Posts list

  * Author Spotlight or Contributor Cards

  * Newsletter Subscription Box ("Subscribe to Alma'rifa Insights")

### 4. Reading Experience & Article Page (`/article/[slug]`)

- Sticky reading progress bar at the top of the page.

- Clear title, subtitle, author profile block (avatar, bio, follow button), publication date, and estimated reading time.

- Floating social share bar (X/Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Copy Link).

- Clean long-form content area with drop-cap options for article intros, blockquotes, inline imagery, and structured headings.

- Dedicated Poetry Renderer: Special styling for poetry posts to preserve line breaks, stanzas, and rhythmic formatting cleanly.

- Author Bio Box at the bottom of the article.

- Related Articles slider or grid at the bottom.

- Interactive Comments Section with moderation features.

### 5. Content Management & Filtering

- Dynamic category filtering and tag-based indexing (e.g., Philosophy, Literature, Culture, History, Modern Essays).

- Pagination or "Load More" infinite scroll implementation for category archives.

### 6. Technical & UX Specs

- Built with React, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide Icons.

- Fully mobile-responsive and optimized for fast page load speed and accessibility (WCAG compliant).

- SEO-optimized metadata structure for article posts (Open Graph tags, canonical URLs, structured schema markup).
